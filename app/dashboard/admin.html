Step Visibility

Control which onboarding steps appear on the Onboarding Dashboard for your team. Steps are auto-configured based on your business type (not set), but you can override any step here. Toggling a step off hides it from the Onboarding Dashboard — your team won't see it or be asked to complete it. Locked steps (Roles & Permissions, Employees, Equipment) are required for all business types and cannot be turned off.

Key Personnel Assignments

Assign key personnel and the onboarding tasks they are responsible for. They will receive an email with login instructions and will see only their assigned tasks on the Onboarding Dashboard. You can assign multiple tasks to the same person or split tasks across different team members.
