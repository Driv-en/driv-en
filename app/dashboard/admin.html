Task Visibility

⚠ You have unsaved visibility changes. Click Save Visibility before switching tabs or leaving this page, or your changes will be lost.

Control which onboarding tasks appear on the Onboarding Dashboard for your team. Tasks are auto-configured based on your business type (not set), but you can override any task here. Toggling a task off hides it from the Onboarding Dashboard — your team won't see it or be asked to complete it. Locked tasks (Roles & Permissions, Employees, Equipment) are required for all business types and cannot be turned off.

Key Personnel Assignments

Assign key personnel and the onboarding tasks they are responsible for. They will receive an email with login instructions and will see only their assigned tasks on the Onboarding Dashboard. You can assign multiple tasks to the same person or split tasks across different team members.

Tip: Add the same person multiple times with different tasks, or add different people for different tasks. Each person receives one invitation email listing all their assigned tasks.
