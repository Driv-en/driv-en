🎯 Module Purpose

Overview & Objectives of Asset Management

The Asset Management module acts as your central registry for all heavy machinery, vehicles, and high-value tools across your organization.

  • Track Deployment: Monitor project assignments, geographic locations, and active operational status.
  • Preventative Maintenance: Log and update meter readings (hours/miles) to schedule timely service.
  • Accountability: Associate specific operators and business divisions with each piece of equipment.

📋 User Instructions

How to manage equipment and inventory

  1. KPI Metrics: Click any KPI box (e.g., Available) on the overview tab to quickly jump to and filter the asset table.
  2. Searching & Filtering: Combine multi-select dropdowns (Types, Statuses, Projects, Locations) with text search for instant results.
  3. Map View: Switch to the Asset Location tab to view active pins with asset numbers, last reported addresses, and Street View links. Use the mode toggle to view Form Activity vs. Project Assignments.
  4. Adding Assets: Use the + Quick Add Asset tab to create new entries. Duplicate active asset numbers are automatically prevented.
  5. Exporting: Download full or filtered asset lists formatted for Excel or CSV via the action buttons.
