Purpose — Carrier Dashboard

The Carrier Dashboard is where your organization stores the carrier-level information that makes your Driver Qualification Files and FMCSA audit packets complete and audit-ready.

What this page holds

  • Carrier / DOT Compliance Info — your legal carrier name, USDOT and MC numbers, SCAC, address, phone, USDOT PIN, and insurance details. This information is printed on every DQF and audit packet so an auditor can identify the carrier immediately.
  • Drug & Alcohol Record-Keeping — a declaration of how your company maintains drug and alcohol testing records (in DRIV-EN, with a third-party administrator, in the FMCSA Clearinghouse, or in a physical file). The audit packet adapts to your choice.
  • FMCSA Clearinghouse query tracking — an optional tracker for the pre-employment and annual Clearinghouse queries every CDL carrier must perform.
Why it matters: A DQF without a carrier name and DOT number is not audit-ready. This page is the single source of truth for that information, and it feeds the DQF printout and the FMCSA Audit packet automatically.

Who should maintain this page

The person who knows your DOT/MC numbers and insurance details — often a safety director, compliance manager, or office manager. During onboarding, the admin can assign the Carrier Info task to that person so they are responsible for keeping it current.

Instructions — Carrier Dashboard

1. Carrier / DOT Compliance Info

  1. Enter your Legal Carrier Name exactly as it appears on your FMCSA registration.
  2. Enter your USDOT Number and MC Number (if you have interstate authority). These are required for audit-ready documents.
  3. Add your SCAC, physical address, phone, USDOT PIN, and insurance carrier/policy for completeness.
  4. Click Save Carrier Info. The information now appears on every DQF printout and audit packet.

2. Drug & Alcohol Record-Keeping

Under 49 CFR 382.401, drug and alcohol testing records must be kept in a secure location, separate from the personnel file. Different carriers maintain these records differently, so tell DRIV-EN which model you use:

  • DRIV-EN holds the records — you enter each test (date, reason, result, document) on the driver profile. The audit packet includes the actual test documents.
  • Third-Party Administrator (TPA) holds the records — a TPA (e.g. DISA, Foley) runs your program and holds the records. Enter the TPA name and contact. The audit packet includes a page stating records are held by your TPA, with their contact details.
  • FMCSA Clearinghouse only — the platform tracks your Clearinghouse query dates/results, and the audit packet includes that record.
  • Physical secure file — the platform notes where physical records are kept, and the audit packet includes a reference page.

Select your model, fill in any reference fields (TPA name/contact), and click Save Drug & Alcohol Settings.

3. FMCSA Clearinghouse Query Tracking — how it works

What is the Clearinghouse? The FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse is a federal database that records drug and alcohol violations for CDL drivers. Every CDL carrier is required to query it: a full query before hiring (pre-employment) and a limited query at least once a year (annual).

How tracking works in DRIV-EN:

  1. Your company performs the query yourself at clearinghouse.fmcsa.dot.gov (you log in with your own Clearinghouse account).
  2. You record the outcome in DRIV-EN — the query date, the query type (pre-employment or annual), and the result (clear / not clear / prohibited).
  3. DRIV-EN stores that record per driver and includes it in the DQF and FMCSA Audit packet.

What you get: a per-driver Clearinghouse query history that proves you performed the required queries — exactly what an auditor asks for.

How the info gets to DRIV-EN: you enter it (or your assigned compliance person does). DRIV-EN does not connect to the Clearinghouse API or perform queries on your behalf — it is a record-keeping tracker, so there is no cost to DRIV-EN.

Cost note: The FMCSA charges $1.25 per full (pre-employment) query; limited (annual) queries are free. That fee is paid by your company directly to FMCSA — DRIV-EN never charges for Clearinghouse tracking.

Turn on Track FMCSA Clearinghouse Queries to enable the per-driver query fields on the driver profile.
