Purpose — Employee Record

This page is where HR / hiring adds a new employee to DRIV-EN, or views and edits an existing one. It captures the information DRIV-EN needs for employment records and, when the person is a commercial driver, to start their Driver Qualification File.

What this page is for

  • Add a new employee with their personal, contact, employment, and identification details.
  • Edit an existing employee (if you have permission).
  • View a record read-only — click any row in the Employee Directory to open it here.
Commercial drivers: if you mark someone as a Commercial Driver, they automatically appear in the Driver Qualification Files (DQF) area so the safety/compliance team can complete their file. You enter the basics here; they complete the compliance documents.
Instructions — Employee Record

Filling in the form

  1. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*): First/Last Name, Email, full address (Street, City, State, Zip), Role, Status, Hire Date, the three Yes/No questions (Commercial Driver, Drives Co. Vehicle, Uses Personal Vehicle/Asset), and the identification fields.
  2. Voice dictation: click the microphone icon next to a text field to speak the value instead of typing. If your browser asks for microphone permission, click Allow. If the microphone does not respond, enable microphone access for this site in your browser settings.
  3. Identification: if the employee has a Driver's License, enter the DL number, state, and expiration and upload front/back photos. If they do not have a DL, choose "No — State ID only" and enter their State ID instead.
  4. Combined license scan: if the front and back are on one image, upload the same file to both the Front and Back fields.
  5. Viewing documents: after a photo is on file, a View button appears next to it to open the image.
  6. Commercial Driver = Yes: the employee is flagged into the DQF for the compliance team, and a notification is sent. You do not need to add them again anywhere else.
  7. Terminated status: selecting Terminated reveals a Termination Date field (used for record retention).
  8. Save: click Save Employee. You return to the Employee Directory.

What gets imported (and what does NOT)

When you use the Import Employees feature on the Employee Directory, DRIV-EN imports only the fields it needs: name, email, employee number, phone, address, role, status, and driver's license details. It does not import sensitive payroll data — no Social Security numbers, bank accounts, pay rates, tax IDs, or benefits. You are not replacing your payroll system; DRIV-EN only takes the identity and compliance fields it uses.
