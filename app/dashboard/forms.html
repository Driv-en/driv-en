Purpose

The Operational Forms hub enables field personnel to record fuel purchases via manual entry or AI receipt extraction, and adjust fuel tank inventory counts with mandatory location capture.

Instructions

  • Ensure location services are enabled on your device prior to submitting forms.
  • Under Fuel Purchase, click Take Photo or Upload File to automatically extract receipt information with AI, or fill out line items manually.
  • Under Tank Adjust, select a tank and input a positive or negative quantity to reconcile shortages or overages. A reason is required.
