ⓘ Purpose: Onboarding Dashboard

The Onboarding Dashboard establishes the core operational directory and master data for your organization in the DRIV‑EN Platform.

By completing these tasks, you configure the foundational relationships that drive your everyday fleet operations:

  • Fleet & Asset Management: Standardizes machinery, vehicle classification, and serial tracking across job sites.
  • Operational Tracking: Connects employees, job locations, and client billing for field work orders and dispatching.
  • Cost & Maintenance Intelligence: Configures divisions, fuel types, and units of measure for accurate fluid logging and preventive maintenance schedules.

Once all assigned onboarding tasks are finished, your organization seamlessly transitions to full operational capability on your live dashboard.

📋 Instructions

Follow these steps to complete your organization's onboarding setup:

  1. Review Your Task Assignments: Check the Assigned: line on each card. You are responsible for completing the tasks assigned to your name.
  2. Check Task Dependencies: Some tasks have prerequisites (e.g., Roles & Permissions unlocks Employees; Asset Types unlocks Assets). Locked tasks will automatically become Ready as soon as their required steps are completed.
  3. Enter Your Data: Click the blue "Open [Task]" button on any active card to input your data (available via single entry or mass Excel upload).
  4. Mark Tasks Complete: When you finish entering data for a task, return here and click the green "✓ Mark Complete" button on that card.
  5. Multi-User Verification: If a task is assigned to multiple Key Personnel, all assigned team members must click "Mark Complete" for the card to finalize.
