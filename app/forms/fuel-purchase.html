Form Purpose

This form is designed to record transactions when buying fuel from third-party vendors, gas stations, or bulk suppliers. Data captured feeds directly into accounting and job costing reports.

Instructions

  • Select your Payment Type prior to taking or uploading a receipt.
  • Upload a clear receipt image or PDF to auto-fill line items and vendor information.
  • Review all highlighted fields to verify correct extraction of quantities and prices.
  • If buying for multiple assets or projects on one receipt, use the "Add Line" button.
  • Ensure required fields marked with an asterisk (*) are completed prior to recording.
