Asset Types Selection Purpose

This page establishes your organization's master machinery and equipment classifications. Selecting discrete asset types configures default preventative maintenance schedules, dynamic pre-trip DVIR checklist rules, and specialized specification schemas across your platform tenant.

How to Select Asset Types

  1. Review each category group and check all machinery types operated by your company.
  2. If an asset type is not listed, type its name in the Add Custom field and click + Add Custom.
  3. Click Save & Continue to synchronize your selections to Cloudflare D1.
