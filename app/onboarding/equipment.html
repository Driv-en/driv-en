Add ALL equipment and assets your organization owns, leases, or tracks — not just items needing preventative maintenance. Include vehicles, trailers, tools, and any other asset you want tracked. Each item is assigned to a Project/Location so its current location can be pulled up at any time.

Required: Asset Number, Equipment Type, Current Meter Reading, and Current Project/Location.

⚠ Prerequisite not met.
Equipment & Assets requires the Projects task to be completed first. The Project/Location dropdown below will be empty until projects are added. You can still add equipment manually, but you won't be able to assign it to a project until the Projects task is completed.
