Add ALL assets your organization owns, leases, or tracks — not just items needing preventative maintenance. Include vehicles, trailers, tools, and any other asset you want tracked. Each item can be assigned to a Project and/or Location so its current whereabouts can be pulled up at any time.

Required: Asset Number and Asset Type. Current Meter Reading, Current Project, and Current Location are optional.

⚠ Prerequisites not met.
Assets requires the Projects, Fuel Types, Units of Measure, and Asset Types tasks to be completed first. The dropdowns below will be empty until those tasks are done. You can still add assets manually, but you won't be able to assign an Asset Type, Project, Fuel Type, or Unit of Measure until the corresponding task is completed.
