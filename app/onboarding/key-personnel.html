Assign Onboarding Responsibilities

Key Personnel are the people who will be responsible for uploading onboarding data. This may include admins, clerks, supervisors, or anyone who has access to the information needed for their assigned tasks. Enter their full name and contact information, then select all onboarding tasks they are responsible for.

You may assign multiple people to the same task, and you may assign the same person to multiple tasks. These assignments determine who is responsible for completing your initial data uploads.

You can save at any time — all tasks do not need to be assigned. Come back later to add more people or revoke assignments.

HR/Payroll will complete any remaining employee fields later.

Onboarding Responsibilities

These are the setup tasks required so your Driv‑en modules can operate correctly. They are not modules; they are data uploads your team will complete during onboarding.

Company Information
  • Employees — employee name and email (required for Work Orders and future Safety/Training modules).
  • Divisions — division name (your company's internal structure used for equipment transfers and reporting).
Project Information
  • Clients — client name (companies or owners associated with your projects).
  • Project Types — project type name (used to categorize projects for scheduling and reporting; someone knowledgeable about your project categories).
  • Projects — project name, client, division, and project type (required for Work Orders, Preventative Maintenance scheduling, and future Project Management).
Equipment Information
  • Equipment — asset number, equipment type, current meter reading, and current project/location (required for Equipment Management, Preventative Maintenance, Inspections, Fuel Usage, and Transfers).

Administrator Information

Your name and email are used to personalize the notification emails sent to each Key Personnel (they will CC you). Required only when adding new Key Personnel.
