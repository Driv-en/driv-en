# Equipment Management Module (v1.0)

## Included
- Equipment Specs
- Equipment Status
- Equipment Transfers
- Equipment Documents
- Meter Tracking
- Fuel Usage
- Out of Service Tracking
- Downtime Reasons
- Status History
- Equipment Attachments
- Equipment Configurations
- PM Scheduling
- PM Templates
- PM Assignments
- PM Completion
- PM Review
- Work Orders (supporting)

## Rules
- Equipment is the core record
- All workflows revolve around Equipment
- Status transitions must be validated by Workers
