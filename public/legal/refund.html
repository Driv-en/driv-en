Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: August 2026
DRIV‑EN is a product of Digital Safety Inspection LLC (“Digital Safety Inspection”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how subscription cancellations and refunds are handled for the DRIV‑EN platform and related services.

2. Subscription Billing Cycle

DRIV‑EN subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis (monthly or annually). Your billing cycle begins on the date you subscribe and renews automatically unless cancelled.

3. Cancellation Process

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

After cancellation:

  • Your subscription remains active until the billing period ends.
  • You will not be charged for future billing periods.
  • Your account access may be limited or disabled after the period ends.

4. Refund Eligibility

DRIV‑EN subscriptions are generally non‑refundable. However, refunds may be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges caused by a billing error.
  • Accidental subscription to the wrong plan (if reported immediately).
  • Service availability issues caused directly by our platform.

Refund requests must be submitted to support@driv-en.com.

5. Non‑Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refunds:

  • Partial months of service after cancellation.
  • Unused time remaining in a billing cycle.
  • Add‑ons or usage‑based charges already incurred.
  • Annual subscriptions after the first 7 days.

6. Account Termination

We may suspend or terminate your account if payments are not made when due or if you violate our Terms of Service. Termination does not entitle you to a refund for past charges.
