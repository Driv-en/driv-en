DRIV‑EN – Module Selection

Step 1 · Choose your modules

Select the modules your business needs

DRIV‑EN is modular. Choose the features you want to activate for your workspace. You can adjust your active modules whenever your business needs change.

Equipment Management is required. Other modules are optional and may be billed monthly or annually.

Available Modules

Core equipment tracking for your entire operation. Manage units, locations, condition, specifications, and operational status. Includes DRIV‑EN’s extraction engine, which retrieves manufacturer‑specific details such as part numbers, fluid types, capacities, and maintenance intervals based on each unit’s configuration. Intelligent automation assists with data extraction, configuration mapping, and provides dashboards for every employee role, showing the inspections, maintenance, and equipment activity relevant to their responsibilities.
DRIV‑EN PM supports everything from crew trucks and light‑duty equipment to heavy machinery, batch plants, and production equipment used in manufacturing facilities. Auto‑generate PM schedules, track intervals, record completions, and maintain compliance across all equipment categories.
Inspection templates are automatically tailored to each unit based on equipment type, configuration, safety requirements, and operational characteristics. Only fields relevant to that specific unit appear for the operator or authorized personnel.
Create work orders, assign authorized personnel, track progress, and close tasks with full documentation and equipment linkage.
