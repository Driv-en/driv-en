DRIV‑EN – Module Selection

Step 1 of Onboarding · Choose the modules to activate for this customer

Select modules

Choose the modules this customer will use in v1.0. We’ll use this to drive onboarding, Cloudflare Workers, and which pages unlock in their workspace.

Equipment Management is required. Other modules are optional and can be added later.
Equipment Management
Required
Core equipment registry, divisions, projects, locations, attachments, and status tracking.
Preventative Maintenance
Optional
PM templates, schedules, completions, and integration with Work Orders for failed PMs.
Inspections
Optional
Equipment inspections, safety checks, and escalation of failed inspections into Work Orders.
Work Orders
Optional
Technician work orders, foreman review, supervisor approval, and linkage to PM/Inspections/Issues.
Fuel & Daily Usage
Optional
Fuel logs, daily usage, meter readings, and utilization reporting for equipment.
Safety & Compliance
Optional
Safety issues, compliance tracking, and integration with inspections and work orders.
