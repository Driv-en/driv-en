DRIV‑EN Admin Onboarding Dashboard

Guide your organization through initial setup in seven structured steps.

Onboarding Overview

This dashboard shows the current status of each onboarding step and routes you to the correct page.

Begin with Key Personnel. As each step is completed, the next step will unlock automatically.

Step 1 — Key Personnel
Checking…
Identify the key decision‑makers and operational leads who will receive DRIV‑EN access and onboarding tasks.
After Key Personnel are entered and saved, DRIV‑EN will create their accounts and email them login credentials and onboarding instructions.
Step 2 — Employees
Locked

Step 3 — Clients
Locked
Add clients and customer organizations that will receive proposals, contracts, and work orders.
Step 4 — Divisions
Locked
Define internal divisions or business units used for reporting, cost tracking, and operational separation.
Step 5 — Project Types
Locked
Configure project types that represent the kinds of work your organization performs (e.g., highway, sitework, maintenance).
Step 6 — Projects
Locked
Add active or upcoming projects that will be scheduled, tracked, and reported in DRIV‑EN.
Step 7 — Equipment
Locked
Add equipment and assets that will be scheduled, dispatched, and maintained through DRIV‑EN.
