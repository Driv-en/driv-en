DRIV‑EN – Onboarding

Guide your team through v1.0 setup in the correct order.

Onboarding Dashboard

Each step shows whether it is ready, waiting on prerequisites, or locked. Admin completes Key Personnel first, then Key Personnel and teams complete the remaining steps.

1. Key Personnel Upload (Admin)
Upload Project Managers, Equipment Managers, HR/Payroll, Safety, and other key roles. This populates the Employee table with the people who will perform later uploads.
Prerequisites: None. This must be completed first.
Open Key Personnel Upload
2. Employee Upload
HR/Payroll uploads the full employee list. This can be done any time after Key Personnel.
Prerequisites: Key Personnel Upload complete.
Open Employee Upload
3. Client Upload
Upload the clients your business works with. Can be completed in any order after Key Personnel.
Prerequisites: Key Personnel Upload complete.
Open Client Upload
4. Division Upload
Upload your divisions or business units. Can be completed in any order after Key Personnel.
Prerequisites: Key Personnel Upload complete.
Open Division Upload
5. Project Type Upload
Upload the types of projects your business performs. Can be completed in any order after Key Personnel.
Prerequisites: Key Personnel Upload complete.
Open Project Type Upload
6. Project Upload
Project Managers upload projects. This step depends on Employees, Clients, Divisions, and Project Types.
Prerequisites: Employee, Client, Division, and Project Type uploads complete.
Project Upload Locked
7. Equipment Upload
Equipment Managers upload equipment and assign it to projects. This step depends on Project Upload.
Prerequisites: Project Upload complete.
Equipment Upload Locked
8. PM Module Auto‑Generation
Once equipment is loaded, the PM module can generate PM Families, Templates, Schedules, and Completion records.
Prerequisites: Equipment Upload complete.
