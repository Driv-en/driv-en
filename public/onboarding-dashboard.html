DRIV‑EN – Onboarding

Guide your team through v1.0 setup in the correct order.

Onboarding Dashboard

Each step shows readiness, prerequisites, assigned personnel, and completion status.

1. Key Personnel Upload (Admin)
Upload Project Managers, Equipment Managers, HR/Payroll, Safety, and other key roles.
Assigned to: Admin
Prerequisites: None
Open Key Personnel Upload
This step must be completed first.
2. Employee Upload
HR/Payroll uploads the full employee list.
Assigned to: HR / Payroll
Prerequisites: Step 1
Open Employee Upload
Employees must exist before Projects can be uploaded.
3. Client Upload
Upload the clients your business works with.
Assigned to: Project Managers
Prerequisites: Step 1
Open Client Upload
Clients must exist before Projects can be uploaded.
4. Division Upload
Upload your divisions or business units.
Assigned to: Admin
Prerequisites: Step 1
Open Division Upload
Divisions must exist before Projects can be uploaded.
5. Project Type Upload
Upload the types of projects your business performs.
Assigned to: Admin
Prerequisites: Step 1
Open Project Type Upload
Project Types must exist before Projects can be uploaded.
6. Project Upload
Project Managers upload projects.
Assigned to: Project Managers
Prerequisites: Steps 2–5
Project Upload Locked
Projects require Employees, Clients, Divisions, and Project Types.
7. Equipment Upload
Equipment Managers upload equipment and assign it to projects.
Assigned to: Equipment Managers
Prerequisites: Step 6
Equipment Upload Locked
Equipment requires Projects to exist first.
8. PM Module Auto‑Generation
Generate PM Families, Templates, Schedules, and Completion records.
Assigned to: Admin
Prerequisites: Step 7
PM Module requires Equipment to be uploaded.
