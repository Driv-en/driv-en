Onboarding Steps

This dashboard shows the current status of each onboarding step. Some steps have prerequisites — complete those first to unlock the next step.

Step 1 — Company Logo
Company Logo
Upload your company logo to display in the dashboard header. This is the first step — only the Admin can upload the logo. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, SVG. Recommended size: 200×60 pixels.
Admin Only
Upload your company logo to personalize your DRIV‑EN dashboard.
No logo
Step 2 — Key Personnel
Key Personnel
Identify the key decision-makers and operational leads who will receive DRIV‑EN access and be responsible for uploading onboarding data. Only the Admin can assign key personnel.
Admin Only
Assign key personnel and their onboarding responsibilities.

Step 3 — Employees
Employees
Add all employees who will perform inspections, preventative maintenance, complete equipment transfers and fuel usage logs, perform work orders, or be tracked in DRIV‑EN. No prerequisites — can be done at any time.
Ready
Add all employees. Employees are required for work orders, inspections, fuel logs, transfers, and other modules.
Step 4 — Clients
Clients
Add clients and customer organizations for whom your company provides services. No prerequisites — can be done at any time. Clients are required before creating Projects.
Ready
Add clients and customer organizations associated with your projects.
Step 5 — Divisions
Divisions
Define internal divisions or business units used for reporting and tracking. No prerequisites — can be done at any time. Divisions are required before creating Projects.
Ready
Define internal divisions or business units used for reporting and tracking.
Step 6 — Project Types
Project Types
Configure project types that represent the kinds of work your organization performs. No prerequisites — can be done at any time. Project Types are required before creating Projects.
Ready
Configure project types that represent the kinds of work your organization performs.
Step 7 — Projects
Projects
Add active, recently completed, or upcoming projects. Each project references a Client, Division, and Project Type — those must be completed first. Even company-owned facilities (e.g., "Yard #1") should be assigned a project name so all equipment locations can be tracked.
Prerequisites Required
Add projects. Requires: Steps 4, 5, and 6 must be completed first.
Step 8 — Equipment
Equipment
Add equipment and assets that will be dispatched and maintained through DRIV‑EN. Each piece of equipment is assigned to a Project/Location — Projects must be completed first. This ensures all equipment locations can be pulled up at any time.
Prerequisites Required
Add equipment and assets. Requires: Step 7 (Projects) must be completed first.
