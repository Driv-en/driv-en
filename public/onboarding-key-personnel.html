DRIV‑EN – Key Personnel

Step 1 · Assign Module Responsibilities

Assign Module Responsibilities

Key Personnel are the first employees added to Driv‑en. Enter their full name and contact information, then select all modules they are responsible for.

You may assign multiple people to the same module, and you may assign the same person to multiple modules. Only modules included in your subscription will appear.

HR/Payroll will complete any remaining employee fields later.

Onboarding Responsibilities

These are the setup tasks required so your Driv‑en modules can operate correctly. They are not modules; they are data uploads your team will complete during onboarding.

Company Information
  • Upload Employees (basic employee records required for Work Orders and future Safety/Training modules).
  • Upload Divisions (your company’s internal structure used for equipment transfers and reporting).
Project Information
  • Upload Clients (companies or owners associated with your projects).
  • Upload Project Types (used to categorize projects for scheduling and reporting).
  • Upload Projects (required for Work Orders, Preventative Maintenance scheduling, and future Project Management).
Equipment Information
  • Upload Equipment (required for Equipment Management, Preventative Maintenance, Inspections, Fuel Usage, and Transfers).

Add Key Personnel

Hold CTRL (Windows) or CMD (Mac) to select multiple modules.

Current Key Personnel

These employees will be added to your Employee list automatically.
