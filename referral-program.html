Driv‑en Referral Partner Program

Earn recurring monthly commissions by introducing companies to Driv‑en — the platform built for real‑world performance.

How the Program Works

You don’t need to sell software, run demos, or onboard customers. You simply submit warm introductions or share your referral link. Our team handles everything else.

  • Submit your contact information
  • Upload your W‑9
  • Receive your referral link
  • Share Driv‑en with companies you know
  • Earn recurring monthly commissions

Partner Earnings

All partners earn recurring monthly commissions based on the customer’s active subscription.

Standard Partner — 5% Monthly Commission

Everyone starts here.

Premium Partner — 10% Monthly Commission

Automatically earned when you have 3 active paying customers. Premium Partners are demoted back to Standard if active paying customers drop below 3.

What You Earn Commission On

You earn commission on:

  • The customer’s monthly subscription
  • All modules they select
  • Any upgrades or expansions
  • As long as the customer stays active

Commission Payout Schedule

Commissions are paid 60 days after Driv‑en receives payment from the customer. This ensures the customer remains active and no refunds or cancellations occur.

How You Get Paid

Driv‑en pays partners monthly via Direct Deposit (ACH). ACH details are collected securely after your first paying customer becomes active.

What’s Expected of You

Very little:

  • Submit warm introductions
  • Share your referral link
  • Help companies understand what Driv‑en is

You do not need to sell, demo, train, support, or onboard customers.

Partner Dashboard

Every partner — Standard and Premium — receives access to the Driv‑en Partner Dashboard.

  • Your referrals
  • Referral status
  • Customer modules
  • Monthly subscription revenue
  • Your commission rate
  • Your monthly earnings
  • Your total earnings
  • Your partner tier
  • Your W‑9 status
  • Your referral link
  • Payment schedule

All dashboard data is private — each partner can only see their own referrals, customers, earnings, and account information.
