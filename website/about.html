About DRIV‑EN

Our Purpose

Driv‑en exists to bring clarity, accountability, and operational control to industries where work happens in the field, in plants, and across industrial environments — not behind a desk. Contractors, manufacturers, pipeline teams, industrial operators, chemical facilities, refineries, and fleet organizations rely on workflows that must be simple, reliable, and audit‑ready. Driv‑en delivers that with a unified operational platform built for real‑world conditions.

What We Do

We unify equipment management, preventative maintenance, safety, training, inspections, project workflows, pipeline operations, and material receiving into one platform. Each module is designed to stand alone or work together, giving teams the flexibility to start small and scale as they grow.

Driv‑en helps organizations:

  • Reduce paperwork
  • Improve safety compliance
  • Track equipment and maintenance
  • Standardize project reporting
  • Maintain audit‑ready documentation
  • Gain real‑time visibility across operations
  • Strengthen preventative maintenance programs
  • Improve equipment life and reliability

Why Driv‑en Was Built

Most operational teams are forced to choose between overly complex software or scattered spreadsheets. Neither option works in the field, in industrial facilities, or in high‑risk environments.

Driv‑en was built to solve that problem with:

  • clean, modular workflows
  • field‑friendly design
  • clear documentation
  • reliable data capture
  • operational logic based on real experience
  • a platform that adapts to the way teams actually work

Our Approach

We focus on operational clarity. Every module, form, and workflow is designed to be:

Founder Story

Founder: Jackie Blood

Jackie Blood has spent more than 35 years working across some of the most demanding operational environments in the country — environmental cleanup, landfill construction and capping, demolition, industrial and chemical facility construction, mining operations, highway and bridge projects, pipeline construction and maintenance, water well drilling, and large‑scale disaster recovery following hurricanes and ice storms. His early career included nationally recognized work on a Superfund project, where he saw firsthand how critical safety, environmental compliance, and preventative maintenance truly are.

Across every industry and every project, one pattern kept repeating: operations failed when systems failed. Preventative maintenance was inconsistent. Equipment inspections were incomplete or lost. Safety programs were fragmented. Training records were scattered. Workflows were disconnected. And the people responsible for keeping operations safe and productive were constantly fighting a lack of structure, visibility, and reliable tools.

Throughout his career, Jackie built what companies didn't have — preventative maintenance programs, equipment inspection processes, safety policies, traffic control plans, site‑specific safety programs, training systems, and operational frameworks. He trained operators, inspected workplaces, managed projects, and led operations. He saw the consequences of missing data, broken processes, and unreliable systems. And he saw how much safer, stronger, and more efficient companies became when the right structure was in place.

After 35 years of gaining the knowledge, seeing the failures, and understanding exactly what field operations truly need, Jackie began designing Driv‑en — a unified operational platform built from real‑world experience, not theory. Over the past two years, he has architected Driv‑en from the ground up, creating a system that brings together equipment management, preventative maintenance, safety, training, inspections, reporting, project management, material receiving, and more — all in one place.

"I built Driv‑en because I've lived the problems it solves," Jackie says. "I've seen equipment fail because inspections weren't done. I've seen safety gaps widen because paperwork disappeared. I've seen PM programs collapse because companies didn't have the tools or the personnel to manage them. Driv‑en is designed to fix all of it — permanently."

Driv‑en is more than an equipment management suite. It's a complete operational platform — built from decades of field experience, strengthened by modern architecture, and designed to give organizations the clarity, reliability, and structure they've never had before.

Driv‑en exists because one founder spent a lifetime learning what the industry needed… and the last two years building the solution.
