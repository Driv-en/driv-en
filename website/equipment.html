Equipment Management Suite

The Equipment Management Suite provides a complete operational workflow for managing equipment across construction, industrial facilities, fabrication shops, manufacturing environments, and field operations. Customers select the modules they need, allowing the suite to scale from basic equipment tracking to fully automated preventative maintenance, inspections, work orders, fuel usage tracking, and equipment transfers.

Equipment Management (Basic Module - Required)

The Equipment Management module is the foundation of the suite. It provides the core equipment record used by all other modules.

Capabilities:
  • Store unit details
  • Store configuration profiles
  • Track locations
  • Track meter readings
  • Track operational status
  • Upload and store documents
  • Supports manual entry for all data
  • Powers all other modules in the suite

Dashboards provide visibility into unit counts, locations, meters, and operational status.

Preventative Maintenance Module

The Preventative Maintenance module automates maintenance scheduling and ensures equipment remains ready for deployment.

Capabilities:
  • Automated PM schedules
  • Manufacturer‑accurate PM templates
  • Time‑based and meter‑based intervals
  • Guided technician workflows
  • PM completion tracking
  • Equipment Status & Readiness indicators
  • PM history and compliance visibility

Dashboards provide visibility into upcoming PMs, overdue PMs, readiness status, and compliance.

Equipment Inspections Module

The Equipment Inspections module provides structured inspection workflows for any asset.

Capabilities:
  • Customizable inspection forms
  • Daily, weekly, monthly, and annual inspections
  • Safety‑critical inspection items
  • Damage reporting
  • Photo capture
  • Inspection history
  • Inspection compliance visibility

Dashboards provide visibility into inspection status, failures, damage reports, and compliance trends.

Work Orders Module

The Work Orders module manages repairs, diagnostics, and corrective actions.

Capabilities:
  • Create and assign work orders
  • Track repair progress
  • Capture photos, notes, and documentation
  • Link work orders to inspections or PM
  • Maintain full work order history

Dashboards provide visibility into open tasks, completed tasks, and repair trends.

Fuel Usage Tracking Module

The Fuel Usage Tracking module monitors fuel consumption, cost, and efficiency.

Capabilities:
  • Track fuel usage
  • Track fuel cost
  • Track fuel efficiency
  • Update meter readings automatically when paired with PM
  • Enhance PM scheduling when PM is active

Dashboards provide visibility into consumption, cost, and efficiency trends.

Equipment Transfer Tracking Module

The Equipment Transfer Tracking module manages movement of equipment between projects, yards, shops, and divisions.

Capabilities:
  • Track transfers
  • Update equipment location automatically
  • Maintain transfer history
  • Support multi‑yard and multi‑project operations

Dashboards provide visibility into equipment movement and current location distribution.

Extraction Engine Module

The Extraction Engine reads manufacturer documentation to automatically populate equipment specifications and maintenance requirements.

Capabilities:
  • Retrieve part numbers
  • Retrieve fluid types
  • Retrieve capacities
  • Retrieve maintenance intervals
  • Retrieve safety requirements
  • Retrieve operational specifications
  • Auto‑generate PM templates
  • Auto‑generate inspection templates
  • Reduce or eliminate manual entry

The Extraction Engine enhances both the Preventative Maintenance and Equipment Inspections modules.

Map View (Included with Bundles)

Map View provides address‑based location visibility for all equipment.

Capabilities:
  • Display equipment on a map
  • Show yard, shop, and project locations
  • Update locations automatically through transfers
  • No GPS hardware required

Map View appears in dashboards when included.

Dashboard Customization

Dashboards are customizable by Customer Admin and Key Personnel.

All other roles receive dashboards based on their permissions and assigned modules.

Flat Pricing

DRIV‑EN uses flat monthly pricing.

There are no per‑user fees and no per‑unit (equipment) fees.
