Platform Suites

Driv‑en is a unified operational platform built from real field, industrial, manufacturing, fabrication, and construction experience. Each suite contains multiple modules designed to work together, giving organizations clarity, structure, and audit‑ready workflows. The Equipment Management Suite will be available in Early Fall 2026, with additional suites launching in early 2027.

Equipment Management Suite

Available Early Fall 2026

The Equipment Management Suite centralizes everything needed to manage assets across construction, industrial facilities, fabrication shops, manufacturing environments, and field operations. It supports any asset requiring inspections, preventative maintenance, repairs, or operational tracking — not just heavy equipment.

Included Modules
  • Equipment Management (Required)
  • Preventative Maintenance
  • Equipment Inspections
  • Work Orders
  • Fuel Usage Tracking
  • Equipment Transfer Tracking
  • Extraction Engine

Map View is included automatically when customers purchase a bundle. It provides address‑based equipment location visibility and updates through transfers.

Easy Onboarding
Driv‑en is designed for fast, guided onboarding. Clear steps walk your team through setup, and mass‑upload tools allow you to import large amounts of data quickly. Regardless of company size, most customers can be onboarded and actively using the system within a short time — often the same day.

Online Help & Resources
Driv‑en includes a built‑in online help center with clear, step‑by‑step guidance for every module. Resources include walkthroughs, definitions, examples, and visual explanations to help your team understand each workflow quickly. These tools make it easy for new users to learn the system and stay productive without requiring training sessions or support calls.

Safety Management Suite

Coming Early 2027

The Safety Management Suite brings together the core elements of safety operations into one place. It supports consistent documentation, investigations, training, compliance, and field‑level reporting across all industries.

Included Modules:
  • Accident & Incident Management (full investigation workflow: fact gathering, reporting, corrective actions, tracking, OSHA recordkeeping)
  • Safety Inspections (non‑equipment)
  • JSA / JHA Creation & Modification
  • Lockout / Tagout (process creation + documentation)
  • Hazard & Safety Observations
  • HazCom / SDS Management
  • Safety Equipment Inventory, Inspection & Assignment
  • Training & Qualifications
  • Safety Records & History
  • Safety Documentation & Field Reports
  • Compliance Tracking (safety‑related)
Coming Early 2027
