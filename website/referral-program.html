Driv‑en Referral Partner Program

Earn recurring monthly commissions by introducing companies to Driv‑en — the platform built for real‑world performance.

How the Program Works

You don’t need to sell software, run demos, or onboard customers. You simply submit warm introductions or share your referral link. Our team handles everything else.

  • Submit your contact information
    Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can create your referral partner profile.
  • Click “Get Started” to continue
    Once you click the button, you will be taken to the next page where you will receive:
    – A link to the official IRS W‑9 (fillable PDF)
    – A secure W‑9 upload field
  • Fill out the W‑9 online
    Complete all required fields, including your legal name, tax classification, address, and SSN or EIN.
  • Sign the W‑9
    – If your device supports digital signatures, sign the W‑9 electronically and save it.
    – If you cannot sign online, print the W‑9, sign it, then scan it back into your device.
  • Need a scanner?
    If your phone cannot scan documents, go to the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) to install a free scanning app such as Genius Scan or Adobe Scan.
  • Upload your completed W‑9
    After you upload your signed W‑9, our team will verify that all required fields and signatures are complete.
    Once verified, your referral partner account becomes active and you will receive your referral link.
  • How you get paid
    Driv‑en pays partners monthly via Direct Deposit (ACH).
    ACH details are collected securely after your first paying customer becomes active.

Partner Earnings

All partners earn recurring commissions based on the customer’s active subscription.

Monthly Subscriptions — 5% Commission

You earn 5% on every monthly subscription purchased by a referred customer. This applies to initial purchases, upgrades, and renewals.

Annual Subscriptions — 10% Commission

You earn 10% on every annual subscription purchased by a referred customer. This also applies to upgrades and renewals.

You earn commission on:

  • The customer’s monthly or annual subscription
  • All modules they select
  • Any upgrades or expansions
  • All renewals as long as the customer stays active

Commission Payout Schedule

Commissions are paid 60 days after Driv‑en receives payment from the customer.

Partner Dashboard

Every referral partner receives access to their personal Driv‑en Partner Dashboard.

  • Your referrals
  • Referral status
  • Customer modules
  • Subscription revenue
  • Your commission rate
  • Your monthly earnings
  • Your total earnings
  • Your W‑9 status
  • Your referral link
  • Payment schedule

All dashboard data is private — each partner can only see their own referrals, customers, earnings, and account information.
