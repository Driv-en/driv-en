Driv‑en Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the release timeline for Driv‑en's operational suites and upcoming modules. Dates represent estimated availability based on current development progress.

2026 — Equipment Management Suite

The Equipment Management Suite launches Early Fall 2026 and includes all core modules required to manage assets across construction, industrial facilities, fabrication shops, manufacturing environments, and field operations.

Included Modules:
  • Equipment Inventory
  • Preventative Maintenance
  • Equipment Inspections
  • Work Orders
  • Equipment Status & Readiness
  • Equipment Transfers
  • Owned vs Rented Tracking
  • Fuel Logs
  • Equipment Documents
  • Extraction Engine
  • Equipment Map View

Early 2027 — Safety Management Suite

The Safety Management Suite expands Driv‑en into full safety operations, documentation, investigations, and compliance workflows.

Included Modules:
  • Accident & Incident Management
  • Safety Inspections
  • JSA / JHA Creation & Modification
  • Lockout / Tagout
  • Hazard & Safety Observations
  • HazCom / SDS Management
  • Safety Equipment Inventory, Inspection & Assignment
  • Training & Qualifications
  • Safety Records & History
  • Safety Documentation & Field Reports
  • Compliance Tracking (Safety)

Early 2027 — Project Management Suite

The Project Management Suite provides structure for project activity, reporting, documentation, subcontractor management, and operational workflows.

Included Modules:
  • Project Setup & Details
  • Project Activity Tracking
  • Daily Reporting
  • Project Documentation
  • Subcontractor Management
  • Operational Workflows
  • Project Records & History
  • Project Requirements (Safety, Environmental, Equipment)

Future Suites (Under Consideration)

These suites represent future expansion areas based on customer demand and industry needs.

  • Material Receiving Suite
  • Timekeeping Suite (not payroll or HR)
  • Cost Tracking Suite
